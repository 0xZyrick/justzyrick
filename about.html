About

Design taught me to see. Engineering taught me to build.

The common thread is understanding how people experience products, how interfaces communicate, and how the underlying systems make those experiences possible.

  1. 01

    Graphic design

    Visual communication, typography and composition: learning how an image makes someone understand something.

  2. 02

    UI / UX

    Moving from how things look to how they work: flows, information architecture and usability.

  3. 03

    Frontend engineering

    Building the interfaces themselves, and learning what a design costs once it has to run in a browser.

  4. 04

    Product engineering

    Owning the whole chain: the problem, the experience and the system underneath it.

I'm a product designer and frontend developer, and some people know me as Zyrick. I started in graphic design, moved into UI/UX, then web development, and went on to build onchain games. I have a biomedical engineering background, and I have built digital health products alongside work in fintech, games and blockchain.

In Web3 gaming I built Cipher, which won the Dojo Game Jam, along with Stark and ZAP FC, a fully onchain football strategy game.

Games taught me how people move through a product: onboarding, feedback and what makes them come back. That is the lens I bring to every product, in any industry.
